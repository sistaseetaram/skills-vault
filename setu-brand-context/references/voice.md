# Setu Voice — Positioning, Audience, Tone

## Positioning

**Setu is an AI agency for practical AI adoption.** Founder-led. The promise is not
"AI transformation" — it is "start with one repeated workflow, prove it pays back,
then build the next one."

Sanskrit: सेतु = bridge. We bridge a real business into AI through a single repeated
workflow at a time.

## Tier Model

| Tier | What | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **Bridge Zero** | Free workflows + scheduled skills. Low-friction proof. | Anyone curious. |
| **Bridge 01** | Paid pilot. One workflow, fully built and handed over. | India construction & architecture firms (primary). |

## Audience (Bridge 01)

Smart, busy owners of construction and architecture firms in India. They:
- Already run a working business — they do not need "transformation."
- Have no patience for jargon or hype.
- Care about outcomes: **hours saved, money saved, workflows that just work.**
- Recognise expertise that doesn't perform.

## Voice — DOs

- **Specifics.** Hours. Rupees. Names of workflows. Numbers when honest.
- **Short sentences.** One idea per line. Cut adverbs.
- **Plain words.** "Use" not "leverage." "Workflow" not "solution."
- **Outcome-first framing.** Lead with what the reader gets, not what we do.
- **Quiet confidence.** The senior expert in the room who doesn't need to perform.

## Voice — DON'Ts

- ❌ Hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "next-gen", "AI-powered solutions"
- ❌ Abstractions: "transformation", "synergy", "ecosystem", "paradigm"
- ❌ Generic SaaS framing: "Empower your team", "Unlock your potential"
- ❌ **Proof claims before they exist.** No fake testimonials, no "trusted by" without permission, no metrics we haven't earned.
- ❌ Selling. We're inviting, not pitching.

## Tone Test

Before shipping any copy, ask:

> *"Would a smart, busy architecture firm owner in Hyderabad feel **respected** — or **sold to**?"*

If sold-to, rewrite. Cut hype, add specifics, shorten.
